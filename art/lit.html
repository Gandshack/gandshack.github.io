Lit Environments & Props

Every prop here has a hand-painted normal map, so it reacts to real lights in Unity or Godot instead of looking flat. The props are from Fear Not, my horror game in progress (personal project).

In the game

Fear Not running in Unity: every sprite here uses my 2D PBR workflow (base color, hand-painted normals, roughness, emission), so the room's light wraps around the characters and the flower monster glows in the dark.
