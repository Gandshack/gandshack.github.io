Hire me

I make pixel art monsters and animations for indie games, lit with hand-painted normal maps so they react to your game's lighting.

What I make

  • Monsters & enemies: design, sprite and animation (idle, walk, attack, hurt, death).
  • Characters: sprites, turnarounds and walk cycles.
  • Lit props & environments: with hand-painted normal, roughness and emission maps for 2D lights.
