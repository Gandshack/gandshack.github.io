Hire me

Ways to work with me

  • Commissions: one-off pieces, from a single monster to a full enemy set.
  • Contract work: ongoing art for your game, by the project or for a set number of hours a week.
  • Studio positions: I'm open to joining a team as a pixel artist, 2D artist or animator, full-time or part-time.

What I make

  • Monsters & enemies: design, sprites and any animation your game needs (idle, walk, run, attack, hurt, death, special moves and more).
  • Characters: sprites, turnarounds and any animation.
  • Lit props & environments: with hand-painted normal, roughness and emission maps for 2D lights.

Bigger than 96×96? Bosses and giant creatures are priced by size. Tell me what you need and I'll send an exact quote.

How it works

  1. Tell me about your game, what you need, sizes and your deadline.
  2. I send you an exact quote.
  3. You pay 50% upfront and I get to work.
  4. You get work-in-progress check-ins and 2 rounds of revisions.
  5. You see the finished piece as a preview, pay the other 50%, and get the full files, cleared for commercial use in your game.
